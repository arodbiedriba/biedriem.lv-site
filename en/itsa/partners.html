The IT Specialists Union works closely with educational institutions across Latvia to help young professionals gain real‑world experience in the IT field. Our goal is to support students of programming and computer systems by providing them with meaningful internship opportunities where they can develop practical skills and learn directly from industry professionals.

This cooperation benefits everyone involved: schools and colleges gain a reliable partner, while students receive guidance, hands‑on experience, and a clearer understanding of what everyday work in the IT sector looks like. We actively support work‑based learning, offer internship placements, and help future specialists take their first confident steps into the industry.

Educational institutions we work with

  • Valmiera Vocational School — programming and computer systems internships
  • Jelgava Technical School — ICT‑related internship opportunities
  • Vidzeme Technology and Design Technical School — programming and computer systems practice
  • Ventspils Technical School — system programming internships
  • Riga State Technical School — IT field internships
  • Riga Technical College — internships for IT study programmes

We continue to expand our cooperation with educational institutions to offer students even more opportunities to grow professionally and begin their careers in the IT sector with confidence.
