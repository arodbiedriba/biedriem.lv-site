Latvian Lawyers Association

The Latvian Lawyers Association (LJA) unites legal professionals, scholars and practitioners with the aim of strengthening the rule of law, improving the quality of the legal environment and fostering professional cooperation between state institutions, NGOs and society.

Our mission

To promote the development of a democratic and lawful state in Latvia through legal means, ensuring professional dialogue and legal expertise in the public interest.

Main areas of activity

  • • Improvement of the legal environment and justice system
  • • Organisation of professional discussions and conferences
  • • Cooperation with state and municipal institutions
  • • Support for the professional development of lawyers
  • • Public education on legal matters
